# Collaboration invitation

AEW welcomes collaboration from researchers and developers interested in artificial life, evolutionary computation, agent-based economics, complex adaptive systems and scientific visualization.

Useful contributions include experimental design, statistical analysis, richer but auditable artificial economies, genealogy/evolutionary metrics, reproducibility tooling, visualization and comparisons with other ALife systems.

Please keep contributions inside AEW's closed-world safety boundary: simulated resources and markets only, with no autonomous access to real accounts, money or third-party systems.
