# Collaboration outreach — Evochora / Artificial Life

Dear Evochora / Artificial Life developers,

I am developing **AEW (Artificial Economic World)**, an open-source artificial-life experiment focused on evolution under artificial economic constraints.

**Evochora = evolution under artificial physics.**  
**AEW = evolution under artificial economics.**

AEW places evolving agents in a closed simulated economy with scarce resources, multiple virtual assets, agent-to-agent exchange, inheritance, mutation, genealogy, natural selection and bankruptcy. The system is deliberately isolated from real financial accounts and external computer systems; its purpose is observation and experimentation.

I would be interested in exploring whether ideas or methods from Evochora and related ALife work could be compared with AEW—for example lineage analysis, mutation/selection dynamics, environmental complexity, visualization, reproducibility and emergence of persistent strategies.

The project is intended as an open research platform rather than a claim that a simple simulation reproduces real economics. Collaboration, criticism, experimental suggestions and independent replication would be very welcome.

Best regards,
