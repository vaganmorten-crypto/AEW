# Scientific overview

## Research question

AEW 0.2.1 is a closed artificial-life model for exploring how populations of adaptive agents change under artificial economic constraints.

The world combines scarcity, bilateral exchange, resource consumption, reproduction, inheritance, mutation, bankruptcy and lineage tracking. The primary objects of study are emergent distributions and evolutionary trajectories rather than prediction of real financial markets.

## Hypotheses

1. Scarcity can create persistent differences in survival and reproduction.
2. Heritable trading parameters can undergo selection when economic success affects reproduction.
3. Mutation can maintain strategy diversity even after strong selection.
4. Changing resource-production and consumption rules can alter which strategies dominate.
5. Genealogical records make it possible to distinguish short-lived success from persistent evolutionary fitness.

## Experimental method

Run many seeded simulations while changing one controlled parameter at a time. Record population, wealth/resource distributions, strategy distributions, lineage survival, extinction events and generation depth. Replicate each condition across many seeds.

## Limits

AEW is a toy model. Its agents, assets and rules are abstractions. Results should not be interpreted as forecasts or evidence about real markets without independent validation.
